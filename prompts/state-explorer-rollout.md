## Implementation

- Shared `StateBlockExplorer`, generic interaction/cache/loading helpers, Districts/Counties/Blocks inspection, named county switching and whole-county population-share cards. Cached blocks remain visible while new viewport chunks load; changing state/anchor or leaving Blocks releases fine data. Existing Michigan compatibility exports remain.

- Michigan's four land-focused display derivatives and schema-1 chunks are reused unchanged. Other states receive shared simplified coast/divider meshes over their saved census footprint. This does not remove all census water areas or add water connections. Alaska retains its continuous frame, including longitudes below −180; the wrapped national Alaska asset is never used for its state explorer.
- A camera-only fix handles scattered island selections: if a multipart county/district cannot fit at the Blocks minimum zoom, its largest projected polygon becomes the initial focus. Overview still shows every part, panning reaches the remainder, and statistics retain the whole selection. The Aleutians West full-county fit previously cropped around open ocean; the new camera selects real block coverage.
- City generation is complete: 4,124 points across 50 states, 185,438 compressed bytes, from pinned 2020 Census Gazetteer coordinates and incorporated/CDP population tables. Outside Michigan the threshold is 10,000 residents and the label is “Cities & places”; Michigan retains its original 91-city selection. Points do not allocate an entire place's population to a district.
- Schema-2 block data shares unchanged geometry across a state's anchors. Small assignment sidecars restore each exact source feature's district ID in the same order, with plan/state/frame/chunk/geometry-hash validation. Counts, populations and source-feature equality are checked during generation. Michigan schema 1 is preserved.
- `scripts/publish_state_explorer.py --publish` validates all 181 accepted plan identities / 200 state-anchor memberships, compressed/raw asset hashes, sidecar binding, summaries, source/runtime preservation and proof identities. It saves exact manifest/index backups and only adds explorer references. No solver runs or saved assignments are changed.
