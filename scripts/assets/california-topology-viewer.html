What is disconnected?

Physical adjacency requires a positive-length shared boundary and disjoint interiors. Corner contacts and common multipart ownership do not create edges.

Inspect a connection candidate

Lengths measure gaps between water-inclusive census-coverage boundaries. They are not necessarily shore-to-shore distances, certified shortest geodesics, or actual bridge or ferry routes.

A district can use a recorded edge only when it owns both exact endpoint blocks. An island-to-island connection can form part of the route back to the main component.

Can the population-aware policy remove the obstruction?

Each source group needs an integer number of districts. These bounds assume divisible population within a group; they do not measure achieved district balance. The 1% group-population budget selects a policy forest and does not guarantee final whole-block balance.

How this compares with Hawaii

The rule first protects source components large enough to support a district, then tests population-compatible groupings before choosing shorter connections. It may chain small islands together. A connected source graph is a starting condition for the optimizer.

The audit independently traverses the full polygon-part graph after adding the selected edges. It preserves every block, including zero-population and water blocks, and leaves physical connectivity separately visible.

Is 1,000 inner iterations a reasonable start?

Yes, as an initial experiment with the fixed normalized optimizer. New York’s four successful reruns needed at most 377, 410, 557, and 779 iterations in an inner balancing pass. That supports starting above 300, while leaving California’s actual convergence unmeasured.

California has more blocks and districts than New York. A larger iteration allowance increases permitted work, but does not make every pass use the full allowance. Runtime and memory must be measured in a fresh California run.

Inner-solver success, outer-center convergence, final population balance, and connectivity remain separate outcomes. New York reached the unchanged 80-step outer limit despite successful inner solves.
