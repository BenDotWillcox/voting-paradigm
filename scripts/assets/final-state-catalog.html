  • Use the selected report and assignment hashes in this catalog, including the corrected Maryland, Virginia and West Virginia plans.
  • Keep water and exclave links visible and labeled. They connect separate polygons under an explicit policy; they are not physical shared edges or travel routes.

Audit scope and evidence

The audit rereads source population records, validates every selected assignment and compressed payload, and independently checks final full-polygon validity and connectivity under the recorded links. It also checks historical graph/source-coverage evidence. It does not rebuild every source graph or redissolve every source block. Exact per-plan checks and limitations are available in the JSON catalog.
